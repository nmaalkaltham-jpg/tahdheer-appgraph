5Simple reviews panel — essentials only, always moving

Rating summary, a continuously scrolling comment strip, and a write-comment form. Nothing else: no sorting, search, filters, helpful votes or store replies. The strip loops forever as asked — it also pauses on hover and has an explicit pause control, because motion that can never be stopped is unreadable and fails WCAG 2.2.2. The duplicated half of the track is aria-hidden so a screen reader hears each comment once. The reviewer's identity comes from the signed-in account, not a name field — that's what makes «مشترٍ موثق» mean anything. Try the star picker and submit; the comment appears above the form under the account name.

5aDesktop — 760px. Drops into a product page sidebar or under the description.
5bMobile — 390px. Narrower cards, form fields stack, all targets ≥44px.
5cMotion off. What a prefers-reduced-motion visitor gets: the loop is replaced by manual horizontal scrolling, the pause control disappears (nothing to pause) and a «مرّر لرؤية المزيد» hint takes its place — so comments 3–6 stay reachable instead of frozen out of view.
4Product reviews — three directions

Same data, same component contract, three presentations. All three are functional: sorting, search, star-filtering, helpful toggles, expand/collapse and load-more actually work — click them. Ratings count up from 0 after mount, so the true value is in the DOM on first paint (and with JS off); star fills, bars and cards animate once on reveal, and everything collapses under prefers-reduced-motion. Review text is real text in semantic <article>/<blockquote>/<time datetime> — nothing important lives in an icon or an image.

4aDesign 1 — Premium Cards. Deep-green summary block anchors it, distribution beside, six category cards, two-column review grid with hover lift and a shimmering verified badge. Warmest and most conversion-led.
4bDesign 2 — Insight Dashboard. Sticky analytics rail: summary, clickable star-distribution filters, compact categories, smart summary with keyword frequency bars. Review list carries search, expand/collapse, empty state. Most powerful for a decision-stage buyer.
4cDesign 3 — Elegant Editorial. Centred masthead, the most-helpful review as a pull-quote, category strip as a hairline row, single-column reading flow with indented store replies. Most memorable, least dense.
4dMobile — 390px. Design 1 and 2 in compact mode: summary stacks, filters stay reachable, cards go single-column, all targets ≥44px.
4eStates. Skeleton while loading, and the empty state for a product with no reviews yet — which invites the first one rather than showing a hollow 0.0.
