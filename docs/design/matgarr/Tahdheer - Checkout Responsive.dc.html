1Checkout — real renders at both widths

Digital-only store, so there is no address form and no shipping step — the single largest friction cut available here, and an honest one: nothing ships, so asking for a city and a district would be collecting data the order never uses. Contact is three fields; a tax-invoice disclosure covers buyers who need a VAT number on the receipt. Money is derived once (VAT extracted at 15%, not added) so the summary, the review row, the inline CTA and the mobile sticky bar cannot disagree.

1aDesktop — 1120px. Two columns, sticky summary. CTA states the consequence and the amount.
1bMobile — 390px. Collapsible summary at top showing the total while closed, safe-area sticky pay bar at the bottom.
