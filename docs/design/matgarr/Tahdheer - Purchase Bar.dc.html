7Purchase bar — responsive across devices

The same six designs at 390 / 834 / 1280. One change makes this work: the bar still spans the device, but its content is capped and centred (contentMax), so a 1280px desktop no longer stretches three buttons across the whole screen — it reads as a deliberate action group. Mobile passes 100% and behaves exactly as before. Every row is a real render at that width, not a scaled-down mock, and RTL — cart sits rightmost, as it will in the store. Note the price and Apple Pay are mutually exclusive by design: three actions plus a price leaves no reliable target, so the last desktop row drops Apple Pay to show the price instead.
